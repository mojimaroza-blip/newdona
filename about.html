ABOUT GA DONA

A story of passion, courage, and the love of good food.

Meet the woman behind the dream

Donna is a wonderful woman. 💛

Ga Dona is more than just a kota business — it is a story of passion, courage, and determination.

Donna is a wonderful woman whose vision and hard work brought Ga Dona to life. She took the brave step of starting her own kota business, driven by her love for good food and her desire to bring happiness to people through delicious, freshly prepared meals.

Her journey celebrates a woman who believes in herself, works hard for her dreams, and keeps building something meaningful one kota at a time.

At Ga Dona, every kota is made with love, care, and a commitment to great taste. We are grateful to every customer who supports this growing business.
